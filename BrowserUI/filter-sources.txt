The host snapshot in release/atma2-hosts.json was adapted on 2026-11-02 from
ATMA2 blocker 0.3.1, rules 1–83 of rules/host-blocks.json (SHA-256
ec33270e74e408b3c9086a0527c2ad7bebbb8b2aa5cc8d5d4f9e3500aa822c24).
The snapshot contains 82,498 unique domains. The iOS adaptation groups the
domains into individual WebKit URL filters, permits top-level navigation, and bundles no
automatic filter updater. The complete desktop DNR exception, cosmetic and
popup rules are not included in this iOS adaptation.
