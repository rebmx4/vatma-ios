Privacy Policy

Updated: October 24, 2026

About this app

This policy covers ATMA Browser for iPhone and iPad (ru.atma.app). You do not need an ATMA account to use the browser.

Data on your device

Settings, website shortcuts, folders, tabs, tab snapshots and downloaded files stay in the app container on your device. WebKit stores cookies and other website data separately for profiles. The developer does not receive this data through an ATMA server.

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or payment SDKs. ATMA does not sell your browsing history or use it to build an advertising profile.

Websites and search

Opening a website, submitting a search or fetching a website icon connects to the chosen website or search engine. These services receive normal connection data, including your IP address and User-Agent, and any information you enter. Their own terms and privacy policies apply. Ad blocking does not guarantee anonymity or remove every advertisement.

Profiles separate local sessions and change the User-Agent and website mode. They do not change your IP address or emulate another physical device.

Permissions and media

iOS requests access to the camera and microphone when an open website needs it. You decide whether to allow access. Media streams come from the website; ATMA does not upload them to the developer. Background playback and Picture in Picture depend on the website and iOS.

PIN-protected page

A six-digit code protects a separate page of saved links. Its records are encrypted locally using Web Crypto. This is not anonymous browsing: the code does not protect cookies, downloads or already open pages.

Storage and deletion

You can delete shortcuts and cookies for the selected profile in the interface. Local data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. iOS backup and diagnostics are governed by Apple settings and terms; ATMA has no cloud sync service.

Support and this website

Emailing a1238585@bk.ru sends the developer your message, sender address and attachments so they can respond. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary personal information. You can request deletion of the correspondence at the same address.

These public support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection data under its own policy. These pages have no analytics, advertising scripts or data collection form.

Policy updates

If data practices change, this policy and its date will be updated. Contact the developer at the address above with privacy questions.
