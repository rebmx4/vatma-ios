Contact

Email a1238585@bk.ru. Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, ATMA build number, website address and steps to reproduce the problem. Passwords and verification codes are not needed.

Website shortcuts

Open a website and save a shortcut on the home screen inside ATMA. Open it again with one tap using a compact toolbar. Tabs and shortcuts use the same WebKit website data store. Sign-in persistence depends on cookies and the website rules.

Video

Start video using the website controls. Compatible media controls are available in Settings → Media player. Background playback and PiP depend on the website, media and iOS.

Website notifications

Website notifications and unread-message badges on shortcuts are not supported in the current version. ATMA shortcuts stay inside the browser; they do not install separate iOS apps.

Managing data

Manage saved shortcuts and website cookies in the browser interface. The PIN-protected page protects selected links, not the entire browser. Removing the app deletes its local container.

Pricing

ATMA features do not require payment. Websites you visit may offer their own purchases and subscriptions under their own terms. Free ATMA access does not make those services free.
