Getting started with ATMA

Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Open a website

Tap plus, enter an address or search query, and tap Go. The website opens using your current iPhone or iPad.

2. Save a shortcut

Tap the add button beside the address. A shortcut with the website icon appears on the home screen inside ATMA. Open it again with one tap.

3. Make room for the page

Saved websites open with a compact bottom toolbar. The Home icon on the right returns to the desktop. Back and Forward become available only when the corresponding history entry exists.

4. Organize websites

Turn on editing to change shortcut names and positions or group them into folders. Use the tab overview to switch between pages.

5. Choose your appearance

Settings include light, dark and automatic themes, wallpaper, text size and app icons. You can also find the privacy policy and support there.

6. Open Incognito

Open Settings and tap Open Incognito. On the white Incognito screen, choose a website device mode and enter an address or search. End Incognito session closes all private tabs. Ordinary shortcuts keep their ordinary sign-ins.
