Shortcuts and profiles

Open a website, select a profile and save a shortcut on the home screen inside ATMA. The shortcut keeps that profile. Changing the profile in an ordinary tab should not change a saved website session. Sign-in persistence also depends on cookies and the website rules.

Device names identify User-Agent profiles and mobile or desktop website modes. The actual browser engine and screen size do not change.

Video and blocking

Start video using the website controls. Compatible media controls are available in Settings → Media player. Background playback, PiP and ad blocking depend on the website. If a page does not work correctly, try disabling blocking for that site and reloading.

Website notifications

Website notifications and unread-message badges on shortcuts are not supported in the current version. ATMA shortcuts stay inside the browser; they do not install separate iOS apps.

Managing data

Manage saved shortcuts and the selected profile cookies in the browser interface. The PIN-protected page protects selected links, not the entire browser. Removing the app deletes its local container.

Pricing

ATMA features do not require payment. Websites you visit may offer their own purchases and subscriptions under their own terms. Free ATMA access does not make those services free.
